\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}\index{Abstract}
\begin{center}
{\Large \bf Abstract}\\
\end{center}
\vspace{10pt}

\noindent
Accurate segmentation of abdominal organs in CT images underpins computer-aided diagnosis,
surgical planning, and radiotherapy. Manual delineation is slow and subject to inter-observer
variability, making automated approaches essential. This project presents a 2D Attention U-Net for
multi-organ segmentation on the FLARE22 benchmark, which consists of 50 annotated CT volumes
covering 13 abdominal structures --- liver, spleen, pancreas, aorta, inferior vena cava, both
adrenal glands, gallbladder, esophagus, stomach, duodenum, and both kidneys.

\noindent
The model places attention gates on all encoder--decoder skip connections to suppress irrelevant
activations. Training uses a combined cross-entropy and Dice loss (equal weighting) with a
CosineAnnealingLR schedule, and follows a strict per-volume train/val split (42/8 volumes) to
prevent data leakage. Evaluation employs a per-volume Dice protocol consistent with FLARE22
conventions, augmented with Test-Time Augmentation via a four-way flip ensemble.

\noindent
The final model achieves a mean Dice coefficient of \textbf{0.8769} and an HD95 of
\textbf{7.59~mm} across all 13 organs, with major organs reaching a mean Dice of \textbf{0.9243}.
Liver segmentation attains a Dice of 0.981. An ablation study confirms that TTA alone contributes
$+$0.06 Dice and that the attention mechanism provides measurable gains on small structures. On
both adrenal glands and the inferior vena cava, the proposed model matches or exceeds published
nnU-Net 3D results, despite operating entirely in 2D.
