\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}\index{Abstract}
\begin{center}
{\Large \bf Abstract}\\
\end{center}
\vspace{10pt}

\noindent
Accurate segmentation of abdominal organs from CT images is crucial for computer-aided diagnosis, surgical planning, and radiotherapy. Automated approaches are needed, as manual delineation is time-consuming and suffers from inter-observer variability. This project proposes a 2D Attention U-Net for multi-organ segmentation on the FLARE22 benchmark, which consists of 50 annotated CT volumes covering 13 abdominal structures --- liver, spleen, pancreas, aorta, inferior vena cava, both adrenal glands, gallbladder, esophagus, stomach, duodenum, and both kidneys.

\noindent
The model adds attention gates on all encoder--decoder skip connections to suppress irrelevant activations. Training uses a combined cross-entropy and Dice loss (equal weights) with a CosineAnnealingLR schedule and follows a strict per-volume train/val split (42/8 volumes) to prevent data leakage. Evaluation is performed using a per-volume Dice protocol following the FLARE22 convention, augmented with Test-Time Augmentation via a four-way flip ensemble.

\noindent
The final model achieves a mean Dice coefficient of \textbf{0.8769} and an HD95 of \textbf{7.59~mm} across all 13 organs, with major organs reaching a mean Dice of \textbf{0.9243}. Liver segmentation attains a Dice of 0.981. An ablation study confirms that TTA alone contributes $+$0.06 Dice and that the attention mechanism provides measurable gains on small structures. Although the proposed model operates entirely in 2D, it matches or exceeds published nnU-Net~3D results for both adrenal glands and the inferior vena cava.
